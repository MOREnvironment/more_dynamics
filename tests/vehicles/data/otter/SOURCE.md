# Otter composition inputs

MSS `CRAFT/USV/models/otter.m` at `cc07579` is the source of the Otter values in `otter_mss_cc07579.json`: gravity and density, lines 90–91; hull dimensions and coefficients, lines 92–107; payload mass and position, `tests/data/hydrodynamics/generate_catamaran_mss.m` lines 61–62; added-mass coefficients, lines 152–157; restoring inputs, lines 177 and 192; damping constants, lines 99–101 and 202–240. `max_forward_thrust` is 24.4 × 9.81 N; `max_speed` is 6 × 0.5144 m/s, both from the MSS file. No new reference outputs are frozen here. The gates read `tests/data/rigid_body/{inputs.csv,matlab_reference_mss_current.csv}` and `tests/data/hydrodynamics/catamaran_matlab_reference_mss_cc07579.csv`.

The composition uses `site_given`, `no_current` or `horizontal_current_full_rotation_rate`, `twin_pontoons`, `hull_with_point_payload`, `scaled_derivatives`, `kirchhoff_full`, `twin_hull_metacentric_equilibrium_draft`, `time_constant_damping_surface`, and `cross_flow_strip+rectangular_section_hoerner`. Its damping wiring maps `restoring_matrix` to `G_CF`; the cross-flow wiring maps `beam` to `pontoon_beam`. The Otter and REMUS compositions use the same `cross_flow_strip` part module with different section-drag parts.
