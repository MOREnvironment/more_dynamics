# Frozen references and parameter sets — the research AUV Marie, one named part "Marie (estimated)"

**One named part.** Marie is data only: one parameter set of the generic `SpheroidAuv`, named by where its data come from — **"Marie (estimated)"** today; later "Marie (measured)" after the quay weighing and "Marie (identified)" from the logs. The hull form is the floating one (`buoyancy_method = floating`), which is exact both at the surface and when every station is submerged, so the dive and the surface phase are states of the same part, not two parts.

**Frozen** files: a changed byte in a CSV or a parameter JSON needs a decision record. The generator is pure Python with numpy and scipy (no CasADi, no `more_dynamics` import, no `MSS_DIR`/`MATLAB_BIN`): a second implementation of the cited equations. The one library import is `more_transformations` (the 6x6 transformation `H`, taken from there as every generator does).

Run: `python3 generate_marie_references.py` in this folder; a re-run is checked with `cmp` against the committed files (deterministic arithmetic). It reads `LAUV_marie.mat` from the vessel-database tests' own data folder (`tests/vessel_database/data/`; a byte-identical copy of the MSS file, sha256 `7eed3a0c4643ad446b6e45db9f74659052cc56866b3bc3a9c540bd5befcadb61`, pinned by the vessel-database tests).

Made 2026-10-10, library commit `8707610` (branch `generic-models-port`) plus uncommitted work of other jobs.

## What the numbers are (and are not)

Marie's **length (2.15 m) and weight in air (34 kg) are published** by the vehicle's operator: NTNU AUR-Lab, "LAUV Marie", <https://www.ntnu.edu/web/aur-lab/lauv-marie>, read 2026-10-10, "Technical specifications" (also: speed 0.5–2.5 m/s, maximum operational depth 200 m). The hydrodynamic file has the same two numbers (the generator asserts it). Weighing and taping the vehicle as deployed (quay sheet) is still the route to `measured`: payload, ballast and antenna change the deployed mass. Nothing else about her mass distribution, hull shape or propeller is measured anywhere on disk. The body is Fossen's idealised hydrodynamic example (a 1.45 m cylinder with two 0.35 m ellipsoidal caps, 0.15 m diameter, no fins, no antenna, no propeller; his own README: "a hydrodynamic example rather than a validated digital twin"). Kinds, from most to least trustworthy: `published`, `published_second_hand`, `bem`, `deployed_configuration` (the vehicle's own configuration file), `derived`, `convention`, `estimate`, `placeholder`. A `placeholder` fills a slot the model needs and nothing measured exists; it is a reported gap, not a claim.

* **Added mass** — `A(infinity)` of the file (frequency entry 36, the 10 rad/s sentinel; zero speed; 5 m submergence), moved from the centre of gravity to the body origin by `H(r)^T A H(r)` (Fossen 2011, eq. 3.24, p. 49), `r` read from the file's `main.CG`.
* **Rigid body** — `rigid_body_form = given_inertia`: the file's `MRB` (uniform-cylinder radii of gyration, an estimate) moved to the origin the same way, so the inertia belongs to the same body as the added mass.
* **Buoyancy** — the floating form integrates the hull profile; fully submerged it gives `rho g V` of the idealised hull's full outer volume (analytic: `pi R^2 (Lc + 2 (Lf + La)/3)` = 0.0338703 m^3), a reserve of **2.11 %** above the weight. **Open measurement:** the file's own polygon mesh displaces only **0.16 %** more than the weight — a factor of 13 apart in the number the floating pitch is most sensitive to (1 % reserve: 7.4 degrees bow-up at a 10 mm aft centre of gravity; 2 %: 0.8 degrees). The declared 2.11 % is an estimate until the vehicle is weighed in water (reserve buoyancy) at the quay. `displaced_volume` is read only by the `from_volume` form (the generality check of the floating form); `buoyancy` only by `given`.
* **Damping** — the file's stored inputs (surge 50 s, sway and yaw 5 s, roll and pitch ratios 0.2): inputs of a recipe, not measurements, and wrong off the vehicle they were calibrated on (see the soundness check of the spheroid type). A placeholder.
* **Hull drag** — 0.63 on the bare-hull frontal area, a different, 1.8 m, fully appendaged vehicle of the same family (de Moraes et al. 2025, p. 9).
* **Fins** — four, in a cross (a hypothesis: the vehicle's own count and layout are not documented); positions and force directions from the vehicle's own configuration file (the simulator's, taken in the body axes: an assumption); area and lift slope the 2007 prototype's (Silva et al. 2007, p. 4, a different 1.08 m vehicle); servo time constant 0.1 s a published value for an AUV fin servo (no measurement exists); limits 25 degrees and 300 degrees/s are the deployed allocator's.
* **Propeller** — no geometry exists. A lumped quadratic law `T = k w |w|` whose one coefficient is fitted so that the modelled hull drag balances it at 1160 rpm and 1.40 m/s (one row of the configured speed table); the other rows are compared, not fitted. At the maximum shaft speed (2340 rpm) the model gives 2.83 m/s against the published upper bound of 2.5 m/s — reported, not gated.

## The second geometry (generality check)

`scaled_torpedo_*` is Marie's body at scale s = 1.6 (3.44 m, 0.24 m diameter): lengths s, areas s^2, volumes and masses s^3 (same density), inertias s^5, the 6x6 matrices as `D M D` with `D = diag(s^1.5 I3, s^2.5 I3)`, times, speeds and shaft speeds at equal Froude number (s^0.5, s^0.5, s^-0.5). Dimensionless coefficients (drag, efficiency, lift slope, damping ratios) and the servo's own numbers stay. The thrust coefficient is refitted by the same rule at the scaled table row. The generator asserts that the scaled body floats at the same pitch and s times the draft for offsets scaled by s (error 1e-14): an internal check of its own quadrature. It is test data only, never a named part.

## Equations (cited in the generator)

Circular-segment area and first moment (Weisstein, MathWorld); station waterline `c(x) = (-z - x sin(theta))/cos(theta)`; submerged volume and centre by quadrature along the three segments of the hull (Gauss-Legendre, 1500 nodes per segment, converged to better than 1e-6 relative against 6000); Archimedes plus the moment balance for the equilibrium; the surge balance of the thrust law against axial drag and the time-constant surge damping with its speed fade (MSS `forceLiftDrag.m` 28-40, `Dmtrx.m` 30, 48-49, `remus100.m` 127, 218); geometric similitude for the second geometry. The generator reproduces an independent blind implementation's printed values (full volume 0.033870295797 m^3, five reserve cells) to 4e-9.
