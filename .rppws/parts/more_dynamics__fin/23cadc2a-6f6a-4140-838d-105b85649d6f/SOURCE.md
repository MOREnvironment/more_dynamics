# Marie stern plane fin

One of the two fins of the stern-plane pair of Marie (`Marie (MGM LAUV_Marie)`, linked twice there). Values and MGM file:line: the
comment beside each value in `params/parameters.py` and the table in the vehicle part's `SOURCE.md`. MGM fin 2 (starboard) and 3 (port) of `LiftingFins`
(`actuators.yaml`): the fin at x = -0.83 m, area 0.0065 m^2, span 0.0875 m (ruler measurement), finite-wing lift law
(Prestero 2001), no drag, no stall, no servo; `lift_axis` carries MGM's `deflection_sign`.
