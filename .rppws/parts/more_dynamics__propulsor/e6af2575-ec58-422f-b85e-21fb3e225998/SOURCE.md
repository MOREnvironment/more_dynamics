# Marie propulsor

The propeller of Marie (`Marie (MGM LAUV_Marie)`) as one `Propulsor`: fixed shaft along x through the CO, speed command in rpm
ranged to +-2340, net thrust `X = k |n|^p` (n in rev/s), MGM `PowerLawPropeller`. `k` 0.00842703 and `p` 2.2633 are fitted to field data
together with MGM's hull resistance (`actuators.yaml` header): not a property of the screw alone. Values and MGM file:line: the comment
beside each value in `params/parameters.py` and the table in the vehicle part's `SOURCE.md`.
